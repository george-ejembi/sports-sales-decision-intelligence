Dashboard Design Specification

A decision-support system, not a sales report.

Three Power BI pages, built to move an executive from "what happened" to "what should we do" in a few minutes. Every visual on every page answers one of three questions.

02

Design Principles

What the dashboard should never lose sight of

Decision over decoration

Every visual should support a business question.

Context over isolated numbers

KPIs should be accompanied by trends, comparisons, or benchmarks.

Profitability matters

Revenue should never be analyzed independently from operating profit and margin.

Growth must be qualified

Sales growth should be evaluated alongside profit growth.

Highlight exceptions

High revenue/low profit, high volume/low margin, declining markets, strong-margin/low-volume opportunities, contribution gaps.

Actionable outputs

Findings should translate into SCALE, INVEST, OPTIMIZE, or REVIEW where possible.
